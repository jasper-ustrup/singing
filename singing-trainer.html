How warmth is measured

The meter can’t feel where the tone sits in your body, so it uses a proxy: how much of your sound’s energy is in the low harmonics versus the high ones. A dark, chesty tone pushes the dot right; a thin, pinched or breathy tone pushes it left. Try the same note with an open throat and a relaxed jaw and watch the dot move. It’s a guide, not a judge.
